How deep is the ocean really? 😳

**Description**
Sinking from the surface to the deepest point on Earth, almost 11 km down. The last thing waiting at the bottom is the worst part.

### Why these titles
- Under 50 characters, so they aren't cut off on phones.
- Each one opens a question the video answers ("how deep", "what made the sound", "older than America?").
- One emoji at the end: it stands out in the feed without looking spammy.
- `#shorts` plus 3–4 topic tags. The first three show above the title.
