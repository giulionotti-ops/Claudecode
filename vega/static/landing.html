Up in three steps

Run ./install.sh on a Linux PC or server. It sets up the app, the service and the extras you choose.

Open Neesmo in a browser, set your password, and add it to your phone's home screen.

Add your agents in Settings, give them jobs, and let them work.

Questions

What do I need?

A Linux PC or server (a home PC works; Windows through WSL; a small VPS keeps agents running all day) with Python 3.10+ and Node 20+, and Claude Code signed in with a Claude subscription or API key.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere new. Neesmo runs on your server and talks to Claude through your own Claude Code.

Do I need a VPN?

No. Use the built-in login and a Cloudflare Tunnel, or keep it on your private network.

Is it made by Anthropic?

No. Neesmo is an independent product that works with Claude Code.
