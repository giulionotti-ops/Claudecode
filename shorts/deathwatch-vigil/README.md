📜 THE LORE
• The Deathwatch is the Ordo Xenos' own Space Marine force: Marines are lent out ("seconded") by their home Chapters to hunt aliens for the Inquisition. They fight in small mixed kill-teams.
• Their armour is black, with a silver left shoulder pad marked with the Inquisitorial "I" and skull. The right shoulder pad keeps the colours and symbol of the Marine's home Chapter (here: Space Wolves, Black Templars, Blood Angels).
• The Marines in the comic wear different armour marks: Mk VII Aquila (Space Wolf), Mk X Tacticus (Black Templar) and an older Mk VI Corvus (Blood Angel veteran).
• Service is called the Long Vigil. Many Marines serve for years, and plenty never go back to their Chapter. (Source: Warhammer 40,000 Deathwatch codex lore; Lexicanum "Deathwatch".)
• "Half a Marine goes home, the other half stays on the Vigil" is a fan line for the idea, not canon. Not checked live on Lexicanum this run.
