📜 THE LORE
• Commissars belong to the Officio Prefectus (the Commissariat), part of the Departmento Munitorum; at least one is attached to every Imperial Guard regiment (Lexicanum: Officio Prefectus, Lord Commissar).
• Their job is discipline and loyalty. They are allowed to execute cowards, deserters and traitors on the spot, officers included (Lexicanum: Commissar). The idea is that a Guardsman should fear his Commissar more than the enemy.
• Most Commissars grow up in the Schola Progenium, the Imperium's orphanages for the children of dead servants of the Emperor (Lexicanum: Schola Progenium).
• Good Commissars also lead from the front: bolt pistol in one hand, chainsword in the other.
• The Ork charge, the dialogue and the rookie and sarge are fan dramatisation, not canon.
