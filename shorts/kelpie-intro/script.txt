The friendliest horse in Scotland is also the most dangerous. How can that be?
This is the kelpie, a water spirit from Scottish folklore. It isn't a monster that hunts you. It waits.
Imagine you're walking home beside a loch at dusk. And there, by the water, stands a beautiful grey horse. It looks tame.
You climb on. And it gallops straight into the water, and drowns its rider.
Storytellers said it could change shape, too. As a handsome man, it gave itself away by the water weed in its hair.
The strangest part is the bridle. The Laird of Morphie bridled a kelpie, and forced it to carry the stones to build his castle.
Robert Burns knew these spirits. In seventeen eighty-six he wrote that water-kelpies haunt the ford.
