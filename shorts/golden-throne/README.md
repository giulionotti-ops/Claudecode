📜 THE LORE
• The Emperor of Mankind was mortally wounded by his son Horus at the end of the Horus Heresy, about 10,000 years before the 41st Millennium. His broken body was placed on the Golden Throne, a huge life-support machine, and has sat there ever since.
• The Throne keeps him barely alive. Psykers are sacrificed to it to feed it with psychic power; "a thousand a day" is the famous figure from the lore.
• His mind powers the Astronomican, a psychic beacon that lets Navigators steer starships through the Warp. Without that "light", ships get lost and the Imperium falls apart.
• Commissars say "The Emperor protects" constantly, and the Imperium treats it as faith, not a question.
• The 15-hour Guardsman joke is a fan meme, not canon. The commissar's put-down is that joke, so he is saying even a short shift on the Throne would break the rookie.
