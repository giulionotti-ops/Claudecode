# How a Dragon Would Hatch Its Eggs in a Volcano

A 55-second vertical short (1080×1920, 30 fps) in a "creature science" style: simple 3D creatures in game-like scenes, see-through and cutaway views, arrows and label chips, big one-word captions, brisk voice-over.

## Structures considered
1. **Problem → fix → new problem chain** (each fix causes the next problem, ends on an emotional turn). Chosen: it keeps a hook every 5–6 seconds, and the last link (the chick never meets its mother) lands an emotional ending instead of one more fact.
2. Life cycle in order, egg to first flight. Clear, but no tension between beats.
3. Myth vs reality list ("Myth: dragons sit on eggs"). Punchy, but breaks into separate pieces with no arc.

## Logline and arc
A mother dragon can't sit on her egg, so nature's solutions pile up (volcanic ash, the right temperature, pores, an egg tooth) until the baby hatches alone and flies on day one.
Setup: the egg must stay warm. Turn: buried in the volcano, every fix brings a new problem. Ending: the chick climbs out alone and flies, echoing the opening shot of the mother on the slope.

## Benchmark
Creature-science shorts in simple 3D. **Learn:** one idea per shot, a diagram over a real-looking scene, captions one word at a time, a fast problem/solution rhythm. **Don't take:** their characters, scripts, topics, designs or watermark.

## Real-world anchors (each line has a real animal behind it)
- Maleo birds bury their eggs in volcanically heated soil and never incubate them.
- Malleefowl test their nest mound's temperature with the bill (about 33–34 °C).
- Eggshells have thousands of pores for gas exchange.
- Crocodile and bird hatchlings use an egg tooth to break the shell.
- Maleo chicks dig themselves out of up to a metre of soil, alone, and can fly the day they emerge.

## Sound
Voice on top, about 10 dB over the music. Light plucked/marimba score with a soft pulse, dropping out before shot 7 (silence before the turn) and coming back on the hatch. Foley: crunch on the crack, soft thuds as the egg sinks, a lava hiss, ticks on the thermometer, pops on the pores, a shell burst, wing flaps at the end.

## Type
Captions: one word at a time, heavy rounded sans, white with a dark outline, lower middle. Chips: small dark rounded tags with a gold outline and uppercase white text, top area. No watermark (the channel isn't named yet).
