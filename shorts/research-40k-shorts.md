# Warhammer 40k Shorts: what gets views (research, Nov 2026)

Source: 266 YouTube results from 20 searches via Agent Reach (86 Shorts-length, under 3 min), plus Reddit thread titles. Reddit itself blocks reading full threads from the cloud machine.

## What wins
- **Sad stories:** "Fate of the Lamenters" 1.3M, "Angron's story is RIDICULOUSLY SAD" 214K, "saddest moment: death of a dreadnought" 183K, "Pity the Guardsmen" 1.5M, "The Last Krieger" 1.5M.
- **Humour and meme dubs:** "The Emperor of Mankind once said" 5M, "lore vs gameplay" 2.5M, meme dubs 0.5–1.5M.
- **Emotional story dubs:** Chaotic Voices, 0.6–2.5M.
- **Plain "X explained in 60 seconds" does far worse:** dingus40k gets 5K–28K. The exception is the Emperor at 214K.

## Topics people click
The Emperor, Primarchs (Angron, Sanguinius, Horus), the Imperial Guard and Krieg, dreadnoughts, Ogryns, the Black Templars.

## Title pattern
A big name in CAPS plus an emotion word, e.g. "ANGRON's Story Is RIDICULOUSLY SAD", "The SADDEST Death in 40k", "Most Misunderstood Primarch".

## Risks
- **The audience pushes back on AI channels:** a Reddit thread asks for "smaller Shorts creators who aren't AI". The hand-made glass look helps; the voice is the weak spot.
- **YouTube's repetitious-content rules:** vary the topics and formats.

## Plan
- Lead with tragic, emotional stories about famous characters, 45–60 s.
- Use emotional, name-first titles.
- Mix in an occasional light or funny episode.
- Next topics: Angron's tragedy, the Lamenters, the death of a dreadnought, the Last Krieger, why the Emperor sits on the Throne, Ogryns are loyal.
