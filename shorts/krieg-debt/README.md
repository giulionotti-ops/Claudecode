📜 THE LORE
• Krieg was an Imperial world that rebelled against the Imperium over its heavy tithe demands. The civil war dragged on for about five hundred years, and the planet ended up a poisoned, nuclear-scarred wasteland.
• The survivors became the Death Korps of Krieg, Imperial Guard regiments that serve as penance for their world's rebellion. They see dying in the Emperor's service as the way to atone.
• They are famous for gas masks, long greatcoats, grim trench-warfare tactics, and total fearlessness: a Kriegsman will march into any fire. (Source: Warhammer 40,000 Imperial Guard codex lore; Lexicanum "Death Korps of Krieg".)
• Commissars are the officers who enforce discipline in the Guard. Krieg regiments rarely need that, since they are not afraid of death.
• "Krieg just keeps the receipt" is a fan joke for the penance idea, not canon. The 15-hour Guardsman meme is also fan lore.
