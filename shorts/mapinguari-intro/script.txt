A giant that bullets cannot hurt, and nobody has ever caught. What is it?
Meet the Mapinguari.
In stories from the Amazon rainforest in Brazil, it is a huge, hairy beast, with a smell so bad you smell it before you see it.
Bullets bounce off its skin. Some tellings give it one big eye, and a mouth down to its belly. Others say its feet point backwards.
Now here is the real mystery. South America really had giants. Ground sloths. Megatherium was six metres long and weighed about four tonnes. Its cousin Mylodon had little bony plates inside its skin, like armour.
They vanished about twelve thousand years ago. Could one have hidden in the rainforest? Find out next.
