# Could the Mapinguari be real? (physics short)

~59 s vertical, Fable Physics. Follows `mapinguari-intro`. Culture: Amazon, Brazil (folklore); told as folklore, with respect.

## Chain
hide a 4-tonne giant -> food (leaves) -> poor fuel, half the energy (living sloths) -> easy prey, armour (Mylodon) -> stands out, algae fur (living sloths) -> tracks, inward-turned feet -> twist: island sloths lasted to ~4,400 years ago, the legend is the memory of a giant that ran out of time.

## Claims and sources
- Megatherium ~6 m, ~3,700-4,000 kg; isotopes show a plant diet; foot "inwardly rotated", weight "borne on the outer digits and the calcaneum": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megatherium
- Living sloths: metabolic rate "half of that predicted for a placental mammal of their same body mass"; fur hosts "symbiotic green algae which camouflage sloths in the trees": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloth (also PeerJ 2018 https://peerj.com/articles/5600: 40-74% of predicted)
- Mylodon: "the only representatives of the sloths to have bony plates embedded in their skin": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mylodon
- West Indian sloths last recorded ~4,400 years ago (Steadman et al. 2005, PNAS), mainland ~10,500-11,000 radiocarbon years: https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/science/big-game-hunters-not-climate-change-killed-off-sloths/ ("over ten thousand years ago" in the script)
- Legend: backwards-facing feet, bulletproof hair, Oren 1993 sloth idea: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapinguari

## Real-animal anchors
Megatherium, living sloths (Bradypus/Choloepus), Mylodon, Caribbean ground sloths.
Cut for lack of a source: tail tripod, daily food needs, claw length, "acidic soil" (contradicted), digestion time.
"Could prints like that look like backwards feet?" is posed as a question, not a claim.
