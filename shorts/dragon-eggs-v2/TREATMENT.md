# How does a baby dragon survive inside a volcano? (remake, physics short)

~51 s vertical short, Fable Physics. Remake of `dragon-eggs/` in the game-model style (animated dragon GLB for the mother and the hatchling, Kenney rocks and pines, volcano, eggs and ground cutaway built in code). Same topic, fresh script with the AUTO.md formula: paradox hook, myth fix, everyday scene, fast mechanism, sourced facts, flip payoff, loop.

**Hook / paradox:** the safest nest for a dragon egg is inside a volcano.
**Myth fix:** the mother doesn't sit on her eggs; she can't.
**Everyday scene:** sitting on eggs when you weigh as much as two cars.
**Payoff (loops into the hook):** the volcano does the sitting. "...and that's why / the safest nest for a dragon egg is inside a volcano."

## Chain and sources
1. **Maleo birds bury their eggs in volcanically (or sun-) heated ground and never incubate them; holes up to about a metre deep.** Tompotika Alliance, "Maleo": https://www.tompotika.org/maleo/ ; Animal Diversity Web, Megapodiidae: https://animaldiversity.org/accounts/Megapodiidae/
2. **Malleefowl keep their incubation mound near 33 °C.** Bush Heritage Australia, "Malleefowl": https://www.bushheritage.org.au/species/malleefowl
3. **Eggshells have thousands of pores (about 10,000-20,000 in a hen's egg) for gas exchange.** Wikipedia "Eggshell": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eggshell ; SciELO review citing Baxter-Jones (1994).
4. **Hatchlings break out with an egg tooth on the snout (birds, crocodiles).** Wikipedia "Egg tooth": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_tooth
5. **Maleo chicks dig themselves out alone and head into the forest, able to fly; the parents play no part.** Tompotika Alliance (source 1).
6. **Two cars ≈ 3 tonnes** is the short's premise (a 3-tonne dragon, as in the flight short), not a measurement. "Too close to the lava and they cook" is the short's own inference from source 2 (eggs need a steady temperature).

- "The mother tests the ground with her beak/snout" (old script): only malleefowl temperature control was sourced, not the beak test, so it was dropped.
