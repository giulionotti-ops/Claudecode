On the shores of Scotland, some seals are not what they seem.
In the folklore of Orkney and Shetland, they are called selkies. In the sea, a selkie is a seal. But on land, it sheds its skin and walks as a person.
Here's the catch. That sealskin is its way home. If someone steals it, the selkie is stuck on land.
But she never forgets the sea. The moment she finds her skin, she puts it on and returns to the waves.
To call her kin, the story goes, she sheds seven tears into the sea.
The real animal behind it? The seal, which hauls out onto rocky shores. One theory adds people in sealskin kayaks, mistaken for seal-folk.
