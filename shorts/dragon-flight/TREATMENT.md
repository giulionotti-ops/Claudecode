# How a 3-Tonne Dragon Could Actually Fly

48 s vertical short in the same creature-science style as `dragon-eggs` (Fable Physics channel).

**Structure:** problem → fix → new problem chain, ending on a twist that explains the world: dragons live on volcanoes for the lift, not the fire.

**Real anchors:** the largest flying animals (Quetzalcoatlus) weighed about 200–250 kg; bird bones are hollow; wing bones of vultures and other large birds are braced inside with struts; gliding always loses height; Andean condors flap about 1% of their flight time, and one flew more than five hours without flapping, riding thermals.

**Shots:** flapping on the slope, going nowhere → a long bone cut open (hollow) → it snaps → rebuilt with gold struts, takes the load → still too heavy → on the volcano rim, the jump → gliding down, dashed arrow → circling up the thermal over the crater with heat arrows → wide soar.
