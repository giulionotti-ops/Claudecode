# Comic look for shorts (cartoon webcomic style)

Skills in this repo: `baoyu-image-gen` draws the panels (Gemini, already connected,
defaults to 9:16), `baoyu-comic` plans the story and panels, `remotion-best-practices`
or the HyperFrames plugin animates panels into the video. Voice: `voice_el.py`.

Prompt base for every panel:

> Vertical 9:16 comic panel. Bold, thick black ink outlines, flat cel-shaded colours,
> warm mustard-yellow and ochre sky, brown desert ground, simple soft shadows.
> Chunky, exaggerated cartoon characters with big toothy grins and oversized weapons.
> Slapstick humour, dynamic poses, flying debris, big hand-lettered sound effects
> (BOOM, KACHUNK). Clean white speech bubbles with black outline and uppercase
> hand-lettered text. Thin dark panel border. Webcomic style, not realistic.

Rules: original characters only, no official Games Workshop art, names or logos;
keep text in bubbles short (Gemini spells short words best); make one character
sheet first and pass it as a reference image so faces stay the same across panels.
