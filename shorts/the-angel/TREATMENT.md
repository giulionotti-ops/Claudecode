# The Angel's Last Stand: treatment

The third film in the series. Style: Stained Glass. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 52 s, voice 1 (bm_lewis, grim treatment).

Same window grammar as the series: one lancet read bottom to top, the sun as the clock, a crack at the turn, and a pane that keeps its own light at night. It follows *The Horus Heresy* in the lore and reuses its Emperor and fallen Horus. The new figure is Sanguinius: gold power armour, long fair hair, great white glass wings. All designs are original, with no Games Workshop art, sculpts or symbols.

## Structure
- **Hook:** the middle medallion, the two brothers face to face on Horus's flagship.
- **The climb, bottom to top:**
  1. The Angel, whose wings open on "Angel" (dawn).
  2. The vision: he kneels before a glowing roundel showing his own death, and rises on "went" (noon).
  3. The brothers meet (afternoon).
  4. Horus offers his hand; the Angel folds his wings and turns his back on "refused".
  5. After a silence, Horus strikes on "broke", the Angel falls on "down" among drifting feathers, and on "crack" the glass splits across Horus's breastplate.
- **Ending:** the top medallion. The Emperor strikes through that crack on "end". Night falls; a red chalice keeps its own light, and a bell rings on "blood".

## Sound
- **D major for the Angel:** chimes rise as the wings open.
- **Darker chords:** a dread chord for the vision and unease at the offer.
- **The fall:** timpani on the strike and the fall, a recorder lament after it, and a chime cluster on the crack.
- **The ending:** full organ for the Emperor's blow, cut into silence, then one bell and an open fifth.

All foley is synthesized: glass, wings, steel, the crack, the hall.
