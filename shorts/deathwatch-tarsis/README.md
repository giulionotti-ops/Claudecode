📜 THE LORE

• Magos Vianco Locard built a biological poison from the Lictor's genetic makeup (Lexicanum: Vianco Locard).
• Deathwatch Captain Bannon (Imperial Fists) died on an earlier mission, so Uriel Ventris of the Ultramarines took command of the final assault (Lexicanum: Bannon, Uriel Ventris).
• Ventris boarded the last hive ship and infected the Norn Queen; the chain reaction killed the remaining Tyranids on the planet (Lexicanum: Vianco Locard).
• The dialogue and the exact boarding scene are fan dramatisation of the real events.
