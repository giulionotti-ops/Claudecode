# Ogryns Never Quit (comic short #3)

47 s, 1080×1920. Real lore: Ogryns are huge, dim, fiercely loyal abhumans used by the Imperial Guard as shock troops and bodyguards.

**Hook:** explosions, the rookie screams "SARGE! Why is the Ogryn CARRYING me?!"
**Middle:** the sarge: "You're his job now." Grunk the Ogryn shields him from fire (commissar: "Ogryns can barely count. But they never, ever break."), counts the Orks ("One... two... LOTS!") and charges them. Night, rain: the battle is over, but Grunk keeps the shield over the sleeping rookie: "Not till little 'un wakes up."
**Payoff:** dawn. "Grunk... you're the best soldier in the Guard." "...Wot's a Guard?"

Built with the shared engine (`../comic/engine/`), run from this folder:
```
python3 ../comic/engine/layout.py
python3 ../comic/gen_page.py page1.txt page1-art.jpg --ref page1-layout.png --ref ../red-wunz/page1-art.jpg
python3 ../comic/gen_page.py page2.txt page2-art.jpg --ref page2-layout.png --ref page1-art.jpg
python3 ../comic/engine/panels.py && python3 ../comic/engine/voice.py && python3 ../comic/engine/words.py && python3 ../comic/engine/sfx.py
python3 ../comic/engine/render.py --sheet && python3 ../comic/engine/render.py
```
Gemini cost: 2 pages × ~$0.135 = **~$0.27**, no redraws. Page 2 panel 3 drew a human face on Grunk and panel 4 has a duplicate rookie, so panel 3 is skipped (Grunk's line plays on panel 2) and the last shots frame only the left side of panel 4. sfx.py hit a DNS error once and left a broken `roar.mp3`; it was deleted and regenerated.

📜 THE LORE
• Ogryns are abhumans: huge, tough humans who evolved on harsh, high-gravity worlds. They stand 2.5 to 3 metres tall and the Imperial Guard uses them as shock troops.
• They are not clever (many can barely count), but they are famously loyal and almost never break, which makes up for it.
• Ogryns often serve as bodyguards, and Bullgryns carry huge slab shields that lock together into a walking wall of cover.
• The smartest Ogryns get a brain-boosting treatment called B.O.N.E. and become "Bone'eads", the sergeants of Ogryn squads.
• Ogryns love "da Emperor" with all their heart. An Ogryn not knowing what "the Guard" is while serving in it is a fan joke, not canon.
