# Channel plan: Clip Rush

## Identity
- **Name:** Clip Rush: a general gaming clips channel (the best Twitch stream
  moments), focused on GTA 6 from its launch on Nov 19 because it'll be the
  hot topic.
- **Handle:** `@ClipRush` (backups: `@ClipRushTV`, `@ClipRushGG`). Check
  availability on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram before you commit, and use the
  same handle everywhere.
- **Don't** put game or publisher names or logos (GTA, Rockstar, Twitch) in
  the channel name or art. Game names belong in titles and tags.

## Bio
> The best moments from the biggest gaming streams, clipped daily. GTA 6
> from day one. 🎮 (Full text: branding/description.txt)

## Look
- Colors: sunset pink `#FF4FA3`, cyan `#4DE1FF`, caption yellow `#FFE14D` on black
- Font: a heavy sans (Inter Black / Arial Black). Titles in ALL CAPS, 2 lines max
- Avatar and banner: synthwave sunset, neon mountains, "CR" monogram
  (branding/, drawn by make_branding.py)

## Content pillars
1. **Wild moments**: chases, crashes, physics chaos
2. **Details & secrets**: easter eggs, NPC behavior, "did you notice…"
3. **Tips**: money, hidden spots, best cars (these get saved and shared)
4. **Fails & funny**: short, loopable, under 20 s

## Shorts format
- 20–35 s, hook on screen in the first second, caption every 3–6 s
- End on a loop point or a "follow for daily GTA 6 clips" card
- Title = hook + "#GTA6 #Shorts"; tags: gta 6, gta vi, vice city, leonida

## Launch plan
- **Before Nov 19:** set up the channel, avatar and banner; post 2–3 "countdown"
  shorts with your own commentary on the official trailers (transform them,
  don't just re-upload).
- **Launch week:** 2 shorts a day from your own gameplay. Speed matters most
  in week one.
- **After that:** 1 short a day, plus one 8–12 min compilation a week built from
  the best-performing shorts.

## Rules (keep the channel alive)
- Use **footage you recorded yourself**. Rockstar's video policy allows gameplay
  videos, including monetized ones, as long as you add your own content.
- **No leaks.** Take-Two strikes leaked footage aggressively, and it's the fastest
  way to lose the channel.
- Don't re-upload other creators' clips or full trailers. Commentary and editing
  make it transformative.
- Mute or cut licensed radio songs in clips you plan to monetize. In-game music
  can trigger Content ID claims.
